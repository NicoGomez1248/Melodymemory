FAQ

Everything you need to know before creating your song.

We want you to understand exactly what you'll receive, how the process works, and what happens after you order.

1.What does my song include?−

You'll receive a complete song with fully personalized lyrics, sung vocals, instrumental, melody, your chosen music genre, and a downloadable file.

6.How personalized will the lyrics be?+

We use names, nicknames, memories, places, phrases, emotions, and real messages to create a song that could only belong to this story.

2.How long is the song?+

Most songs run between 2:30 and 3:45, depending on the story and structure — long enough to tell it properly, short enough to replay often.

7.How does the guarantee work?+

If something goes wrong on our end — like a factual detail we got wrong from what you provided — we'll fix it at no cost, per our guarantee policy.

3.How long does delivery take?−

Your song will be delivered in less than 24 hours after we receive all the information needed to create it.

8.Can I request changes after receiving it?−

At this time we don't include changes after delivery, by design. Errors attributable to Melody Memory will be corrected at no cost.

4.Can I choose the genre and voice?+

Yes — you'll pick from several genres and vocal styles during the questionnaire, including male, female, or duet vocals.

9.Is artificial intelligence used?+

We use technology to speed up parts of the process, but every song receives professional creative review before it's delivered to you.

5.Can I create a song in another language?+

Yes — you can request your song in a different language during the questionnaire, and we'll match the lyrics and vocal delivery to it.

10.How do you protect my memories and personal data?+

Your story is used only to create your song. We don't sell or share your personal information — see our Privacy Policy for full details.
